About this database — methodology, sources and citation guidance

Data collection methodology

Incidents were identified and verified using Spanish and French government publications, judicial records, and peer-reviewed secondary sources. Each entry was cross-referenced against at least one primary source. Source links are provided in each incident’s detail panel.

Known limitations

Some incidents in the early decades (1961–1969) may be incomplete due to limited documentation under the Franco dictatorship. Casualty figures reflect those attributed directly to ETA and may differ from other published totals depending on classification methodology.

How to cite

Loosemore, K. ETA Attacks Database 1961–2010. kloosemore.com/database/. [Date accessed].

Esta base de datos recoge los atentados documentados de ETA entre 1961 y 2010, incluyendo información sobre víctimas mortales, heridos, secuestros, métodos y objetivos. Los datos proceden de fuentes primarias del gobierno español y francés. La base de datos puede filtrarse por año, método, objetivo y ubicación, y los resultados pueden exportarse en formato CSV.
